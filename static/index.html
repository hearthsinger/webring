Welcome to Rings

rings is an open-source webring and toolkit for member sites. rings was created in mid-2026 as a new webring for blogs, personal sites, and other indieweb pages. all sites in the ring must be created and maintained without the aid of generative AI. in addition to maintaining a list of our members, the rings backend supports a simple integration framework allowing members to host or configure connections to other sites around the internet. view our members page to browse the ring.

rings is accepting new pages. to join, submit your site and a short blurb in a pull request at the ring's repository.
